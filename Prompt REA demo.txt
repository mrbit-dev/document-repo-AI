## 3. REVERSE ENGINEER ANIMATIONS — HIGHEST PRIORITY

This is the most important part of the task.

Observe the website while scrolling slowly and identify every animation.

Pay particular attention to:

- Scroll-triggered animations.
- Scroll-linked animations.
- GSAP and ScrollTrigger behavior, if present.
- Parallax movement.
- Pinned sections.
- Horizontal scrolling.
- Text reveal animations.
- Image scaling and transitions.
- Fade-in and fade-out effects.
- Blur and opacity transitions.
- Staggered animations.
- 3D transformations.
- Camera movement.
- WebGL and Canvas effects.
- Mouse-following interactions.
- Hover animations.
- Page loading animations.
- Smooth scrolling.
- Section-to-section transitions.

1. What element moves?
2. What triggers the animation?
3. What is its initial state?
4. What is its final state?
5. How does it behave during scrolling?
6. Does it reverse when scrolling upward?
7. Is it synchronized with scroll progress?
8. Does it use pinning or parallax?
